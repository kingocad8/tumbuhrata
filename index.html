Frequently asked

Is this a diagnosis or a medical screening?

No. TumbuhRata is a set of parent tools built on the CDC's milestone checklists. The CDC itself says its “Learn the Signs. Act Early.” materials are not a substitute for standardized, validated developmental screening tools. The app helps you observe and keep notes — your child's doctor does the diagnosing.

Which milestone standard do you use?

The CDC “Learn the Signs. Act Early.” checklists, revised in February 2022 together with the AAP. Checkpoints are 12, 15, 18, 24, 30, and 36 months, and every item is something 75% or more of children can do by that age. Items are shown in the CDC's own wording.

What if my child isn't meeting a milestone?

The CDC's advice is direct: “You know your child best. If your child is not meeting the milestones for his or her age… talk to your child's doctor and share your concerns. Don't wait. Acting early can make a real difference!” The Results tab turns your answers into a written list you can bring to that conversation, and the AAP recommends formal developmental screening at 9, 18, and 30 months (autism screening at 18 and 24 months) — ask your doctor about it.

What ages does it cover?

Ages 1–3 years. Milestone checkpoints run 12–36 months; Learning activities are split into 1–2 years and 2–3 years so they match what your child can actually do.

Basic or Complete — what's the difference?
